Thank you for reviewing Skim. This build addresses every item from the previous macOS review (submission ee862d39-828d-4dc9-80cd-260068a9887a):

Guideline 2.4.5(i), sandboxing: the main app no longer carries the com.apple.security.inherit entitlement. Only the helper executables that Skim launches (its on-device AI helpers) are signed with com.apple.security.app-sandbox plus com.apple.security.inherit, as described in QA1773.

Guideline 2.1(a), Help menu: the default Help menu, which pointed to a help book that did not exist, is replaced. Help > Skim Support opens https://gm2211.github.io/skim/#support and Help > Report an Issue opens https://github.com/gm2211/skim/issues in the browser.

Guideline 1.5, Support URL: the Support URL is https://gm2211.github.io/skim/#support. That page has a Support section explaining how to contact the developer.

Guideline 3.1.1, In-App Purchase: the App Store version no longer accepts API keys of any kind. Skim is free, sells nothing, has no paid tiers and does not link to or encourage any purchase, and every reading feature works without AI. AI is optional and off until the user turns it on. It runs on device at no cost and with no account (Apple Foundation Models, MLX, llama.cpp, DeepSeek, or Ollama on the user's own machine), or through sign-in to a Claude Pro/Max, ChatGPT Plus/Pro or SuperGrok plan. Each of those plans is sold through In-App Purchase in its provider's own App Store app (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok), the case your May 7 message describes as allowed.

How to test (no account needed): open Discover and follow any feed, or use Import OPML there. Open an article to read it. To try AI without an account, open Settings > AI and choose Apple Foundation Models (macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence on) or an on-device MLX model, then use Summarize or Ask on an article.
